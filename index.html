QUICK HELP

HOW TO PLAY

Move: WASD or arrow keys. On iPad/touch devices, use the left joystick.
Aim: Move your mouse, or use the right joystick on iPad.
Shoot: Auto Shoot can be switched on/off in Settings. When ON, your weapon locks onto the nearest enemy and fires automatically. When OFF, aim manually and hold left-click on desktop or use FIRE on iPad.
Dash: Press Space on desktop, or tap DASH on iPad. It has a short cooldown.
Level up: Collect purple XP shards and choose one of three upgrades.
Boss: The Void Titan arrives at 45 seconds. Survive to 60 seconds to win.
Coins: Earn coins from kills, the boss, and surviving. Spend them in the Shop.
Loadout: Buy gear in Shop, then equip and upgrade it in Character.
Build ideas: Fire build, steel-orbit build, explosive build, crit build, or mix everything into complete nonsense 🔥
